# General Operations Playbook Notes

This document intentionally contains broad operational language with low diagnostic density.
It is part of the transparent stress corpus used to evaluate retrieval robustness.

## Daily Review

Teams should review service dashboards, deployment calendars, ownership information, alert routing,
and the general incident timeline. A good incident note mentions customer impact, service owners,
recent changes, current mitigation, and expected recovery time.

## Common Follow Up

After a user-facing problem, teams often schedule a review, update runbooks, clean old alerts,
check dependency ownership, and improve dashboard labels. These actions are useful but usually do
not identify a specific root cause for a concrete error code.
